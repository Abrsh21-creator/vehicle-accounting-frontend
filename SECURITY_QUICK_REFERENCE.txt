================================
🗑️ SOFT DELETE WORKFLOW
================================

Step 1: Admin clicks delete button
  └─ ReasonModal appears

Step 2: Admin enters reason
  └─ "Duplicate entry found"

Step 3: Confirm deletion
  └─ Record moves to pendingDeletions
  └─ Audit log created
  └─ 3-day countdown starts

Step 4a: Restore (within 3 days)
  └─ Click "Restore" button
  └─ Record returns to history
  └─ Audit log created

Step 4b: Auto-delete (after 3 days)
  └─ Permanent deletion on next load
  └─ Audit log created
